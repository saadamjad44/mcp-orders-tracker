## FAQ

**What is an MCP server?**
A small program that lets Claude safely read your data through tools I define. Your data stays where it is.

**Do I need a Claude subscription?**
Yes. Claude Desktop with a Pro plan ($20/month) is the simplest way to use it.

**Is my data safe?**
The server runs on your own computer, uses your own credentials, and is read-only unless you order the Premium package.

**Can it update my data too?**
Yes, in the Premium package. Every change shows a preview and asks for your confirmation first.

**Which data sources do you support?**
Google Sheets, Excel, CSV, SQL databases, Notion, Airtable and Shopify. Ask me about anything else.
